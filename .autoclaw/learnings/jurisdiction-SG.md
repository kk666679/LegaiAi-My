# Singapore — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `SG`
- **Official name:** Republic of Singapore
- **Legal tradition:** Common law (English-derived), with significant statutory
  modification and local judicial development
- **Official languages:** English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil (English is the
  primary language of legislation and judiciary)
- **Capital:** Singapore

## Legal system overview

Singapore retains a **common law system** inherited from British colonial rule:
1. **Constitution** — Supreme law (1963, amended)
2. **Statutes** — Acts of Parliament, subsidiary legislation
3. **Common law** — judicial precedent remains binding (Privy Council appeals
   abolished 1994; Supreme Court is final appellate court)
4. **Islamic law** — administered through the Administration of Muslim Law
   Act for personal matters of Muslims
5. **Court structure** — Supreme Court (High Court, Court of Appeal),
   State Courts, Family Courts, Syariah Court

## Official gazettes

- **Singapore Government Gazette** — publication of Acts, subsidiary
  legislation, and government notices (access via AGC portal)

## Numbering conventions

Singapore uses **year + sequential Act number**:
- **Act X of YYYY** or **Cap. X** (chapter number in revised edition)
- **Chapter numbers** in the Statutes of Singapore (revised editions)
- Subsidiary legislation: **GN** (Gazette Notification) numbers, **S**
  numbers, or **L.N.** (Legal Notification) numbers

## Official languages

- **English** is the primary and authoritative language of legislation
- **Malay, Mandarin, Tamil** are official languages but legislation is
  published primarily in English
- **Bilingual support:** Some statutes have unofficial translations; English
  is authoritative

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Official bilingual policy exists but most legal texts are English-only
- Syariah law matters may have Malay texts

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "SG"`
- Primary source: sso.agc.gov.sg (Statutes Online)
- Use `Cap.` number or `Act` number for exact retrieval
- Verify current status via the AGC portal
- Singapore's revised editions consolidate amendments into single Acts

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Singapore legislation** from **giving
legal advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate Act numbers, section numbers, or legal status.
