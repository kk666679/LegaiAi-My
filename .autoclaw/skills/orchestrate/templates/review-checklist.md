# Review Checklist

Before approving any item, confirm:

- [ ] The completion signal lists every artifact touched.
- [ ] Hashes match for files under `kg/`, `spine/`, `vector/`.
- [ ] No writes outside the item's declared scope.
- [ ] No new dependencies added without a corresponding board item.
- [ ] Any new insight was appended to `learnings/`.
- [ ] `safety/mode` was `active` for the entire duration.
- [ ] If the item touched i18n, both EN and MS variants were produced.

Reject on any unchecked box. Do not "note and approve".
