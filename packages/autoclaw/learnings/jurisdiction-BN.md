# Brunei Darussalam — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `BN`
- **Official name:** Negara Brunei Darussalam
- **Legal tradition:** Mixed — common law heritage (English) overlaid with
  Islamic law (Syariah) for personal/family matters and Malay customary law
  (Adat)
- **Official language:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi scripts)
- **Legislative authority:** His Majesty the Sultan and Yang Di-Pertuan
  (legislative, executive, and judicial authority vested in the Sultan)

## Legal system overview

Brunei's legal system is a unique blend of:
1. **Common law** — inherited from British protectorate status; English
   commercial and procedural rules heavily influential
2. **Islamic law (Syariah)** — applicable to Muslims in personal/family matters,
   inheritance, and certain criminal offences
3. **Customary law (Adat)** — traditional Malay customs, especially in land
   and community matters

The **Supreme Court** comprises the High Court and Court of Appeal. A separate
**Syariah Court** system handles Islamic personal law.

## Official gazettes

- **GNB (Gazette Negara Brunei)** — official gazette for legislation,
  subsidiary legislation, and government notices

## Numbering conventions

Brunei uses chapter-based numbering for Acts:
- **Chapter numbers** (e.g., "Chapter 22" or "Cap. 22") rather than sequential
  act numbers in the Malaysian style
- Subsidiary legislation (e.g., S.R., P.U.) follows the parent Act chapter
- The AGC portal organizes laws by chapter rather than year+number

## Official languages

- **Malay** is the sole official language of legislation
- **English** versions exist for many commercial statutes and are often used
  in practice, especially for banking, finance, and international trade
- **Bilingual support:** Some laws have both Malay and English texts; the
  authoritative text depends on the specific Act

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Most legislation is published in Malay
- English translations/explanatory notes exist for key commercial legislation
- Courts may reference English common law authorities

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "BN"`
- Query for `chapter` number rather than act number where applicable
- Verify current status against the GNB
- Cross-reference with the AGC portal for the latest revised versions
- Islamic law matters require separate Syariah Court database retrieval

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Brunei legislation** from **giving
legal advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate chapter numbers, section numbers, or legal status.
