# Vietnam — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `VN`
- **Official name:** Socialist Republic of Vietnam (Cộng hòa Xã hội chủ
  nghĩa Việt Nam)
- **Legal tradition:** Civil law (Socialist), influenced by French civil law,
  Soviet legal theory, and now transitioning to a market economy framework
- **Official language:** Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt)
- **Capital:** Hanoi

## Legal system overview

Vietnam's legal system is a **socialist civil law system**:
1. **Constitution** — 2013 Constitution (current); 1992 Constitution (prior)
2. **Codes** — Civil Code (2015), Penal Code (2015), Civil Procedure Code,
   Criminal Procedure Code, Labor Code, Enterprise Law
3. **Court structure** — Supreme People's Court, High People's Courts,
   Provincial People's Courts, District People's Courts
4. **Party influence** — Communist Party of Vietnam leadership; laws must align
   with Party policy
5. **Economic transition** — loi moi (renovation) reforms since 1986 created
   a "socialist-oriented market economy"

## Official gazettes

- **Công báo (Official Gazette)** — promulgation of laws, ordinances,
  decrees, and other normative acts

## Numbering conventions

Vietnamese legislation uses:
- **Law No. XX/YYYY-CP or No. XX/YYYY-NQ** — Laws passed by the National
  Assembly
- **Decree No. XX/YYYY-NĐ-CP** — Government Decrees
- **Decision No. XX/YYYY-QĐ-TTg** — Prime Ministerial Decisions
- **Circular No. XX/YYYY-TT-BTC (etc.)** — Ministerial Circulars

## Official languages

- **Vietnamese** is the sole official language
- **English** translations exist for many commercial, investment, and
  tax laws
- **French** historical influence but not an official legislative language
- **Bilingual support:** Investment and commercial laws often have official
  English versions

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Many investment and commercial laws have official English translations
- The National Assembly database may provide bilingual access
- Unofficial translations are widely available

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "VN"`
- Primary source: Official Gazette (congbao.chinhphu.vn) or National Assembly
  database
- Verify law type (Law, Decree, Decision, Circular, etc.)
- Civil Code uses article numbering
- Treat Vietnamese texts as authoritative; English translations as secondary

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Vietnamese legislation** from **giving
legal advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate law numbers, article numbers, or legal status.
