# ASEAN Charter — Regional Legal Framework

## Identity

- **Document type:** Treaty / Charter
- **Title:** ASEAN Charter
- **Adopted:** 20 November 2007, 13th ASEAN Summit, Singapore
- **Entered into force:** 15 December 2008
- **Languages:** All ASEAN languages; English is the working language
- **Depositary:** Secretary-General of ASEAN

## Overview

The ASEAN Charter is the **foundational treaty** of the Association of
Southeast Asian Nations. It transformed ASEAN from a loose regional forum into
a legal entity with international legal personality.

### Key purposes (Article 1)

1. Maintain and enhance peace, security, and stability
2. Enhance economic growth, social progress, and cultural development
3. Promote active collaboration and mutual assistance
4. Provide assistance to each other in the form of training and research
5. Maintain close and beneficial cooperation with external partners
6. Promote ASEAN as a balanced, harmonious, and dynamic community

### Principles (Article 2)

- Respect for independence, sovereignty, equality, territorial integrity,
  and national identity
- Non-interference in internal affairs
- Peaceful settlement of disputes
- Renunciation of the threat or use of force
- Effective cooperation among members
- Respect for human rights and fundamental freedoms

## Structure of the ASEAN Community

The ASEAN Charter established three pillars:

| Pillar | Blueprint | Focus |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **ASEAN Political-Security Community (APSC)** | APSC Blueprint 2009 | Political and security cooperation |
| **ASEAN Economic Community (AEC)** | AEC Blueprint 2015 | Economic integration |
| **ASEAN Socio-Cultural Community (ASCC)** | ASCC Blueprint 2009 | Social and cultural cooperation |

## Institutions established by the Charter

| Institution | Head | Seat | Role |
|-------------|------|------|------|
| ASEAN Summit | Rotating Chair | Varies | Highest decision-making body |
| ASEAN Coordinating Council | Foreign Ministers | Varies | Coordinate implementation |
| ASEAN Community Councils | Sectoral Ministers | Varies | Oversee three pillars |
| ASEAN Secretariat | Secretary-General | Jakarta | Administrative body |
| Committee of Permanent Representatives (CPR) | Permanent Representatives | Jakarta | Monitor implementation |
| ASEAN Intergovernmental Commission on Human Rights (AICHR) | Representatives | Varies | Human rights promotion |
| ASEAN Commission on the Promotion and Protection of the Rights of Women and Children (ACWC) | Representatives | Varies | Women/children rights |

## Membership (Article 4)

The Charter originally confirmed the 10 founding-era member
states. **Timor-Leste was admitted as the 11th member state on
26 October 2025** (47th ASEAN Summit, Kuala Lumpur) and
acceded to the Charter on the same occasion. Article 4 provides
the framework for accession of new members.

## ASEAN legal instruments

The Charter provides for the adoption of legal instruments by ASEAN:

- **ASEAN Agreements** — binding treaties
- **Declarations** — political commitments
- **Instruments** — guidelines, plans of action, etc.

### Key treaties and agreements

| Agreement | Date | Subject |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Treaty of Amity and Cooperation (TAC) | 1976 | Peaceful relations, non-interference |
| Treaty on the Southeast Asia Nuclear-Weapon-Free Zone (SEANWFZ) | 1995 | Nuclear-free zone |
| ASEAN Free Trade Area (AFTA) | 1992 | Trade liberalization |
| ASEAN Framework Agreement on Services (AFAS) | 1995 | Services liberalization |
| ASEAN Comprehensive Investment Agreement (ACIA) | 2009 | Investment protection |
| ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement (ATIGA) | 2009 | Goods trade |
| ASEAN Protocol on Enhanced Dispute Settlement Mechanism | 2004 | Dispute settlement |

## Dispute settlement mechanisms

The Charter and subsequent protocols establish dispute settlement:

### ASEAN Political-Security Community

- **ASEAN Ministerial Meeting** — initial diplomatic consultation
- **ASEAN Summit** — political resolution
- **Treaty of Amity and Cooperation (TAC)** — provides for peaceful settlement
  through good offices, mediation, and negotiation (Article 13-14)

### ASEAN Economic Community

- **Enhanced Dispute Settlement Mechanism (EDSM)** — Protocol of 2004
- **Consultations** → **Mediation** → **Panel** → **Appellate Body**
- Implementation through national authorities

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **ASEAN regional law** from **national law**:
- ASEAN agreements are **binding treaties** but require **domestic
  incorporation** to have direct effect in most member states
- Always identify whether a rule is an ASEAN treaty, an ASEAN instrument,
  or a national implementing measure
- Never present ASEAN aspirations or blueprints as binding national law

When ASEAN legal status cannot be verified, the system returns:  
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*
