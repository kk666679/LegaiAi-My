# Lao PDR — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `LA`
- **Official name:** Lao People's Democratic Republic (Lao PDR)
- **Legal tradition:** Civil law (French-derived), with socialist legal
  influences and customary elements
- **Official language:** Lao
- **Capital:** Vientiane

## Legal system overview

Lao PDR operates a **civil law system** based on French legal tradition, adapted
to a socialist-oriented market economy:
1. **Constitution** — 1991 Constitution (amended 2003, 2015)
2. **Codes** — Civil Code, Penal Code, Civil Procedure Code, Criminal
   Procedure Code
3. **People's Court** — Supreme People's Court at the apex
4. **Socialist influences** — Party leadership, state-owned enterprise regulation

## Official gazettes

- **Official Gazette of the Lao PDR** — promulgation of laws, decrees,
  decisions, and ordinances

## Numbering conventions

Lao legislation uses:
- **Year + sequential number** within each type
- **Presidential/Prime Ministerial decrees** (No. X/PM or No. X/PR)
- **National Assembly laws** (No. X/NA)
- **Ministerial ordinances** (No. X/MO)

## Official languages

- **Lao** is the sole official language
- **French** historical influence but not an official legislative language
- **English** translations are rare and usually unofficial
- **Bilingual support:** Minimal; most authoritative texts are in Lao only

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Some investment and trade laws may have English summaries
- Unofficial translations by international organizations exist

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "LA"`
- Verify source against Official Gazette or Ministry of Justice
- Treat Lao texts as authoritative; translations as secondary
- Laws are often referred to by year and decree number
- The official gazette has no verified stable public URL — confirm the
  access path via the Ministry of Justice before citing a gazette URL

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Lao legislation** from **giving legal
advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate law numbers, article numbers, or legal status.
