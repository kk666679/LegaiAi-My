# Myanmar — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `MM`
- **Official name:** Republic of the Union of Myanmar
- **Legal tradition:** Mixed — common law heritage (English) overlaid with
  customary law and post-1948/1974/2008 constitutional influences
- **Official language:** Burmese (Myanmar)
- **Capital:** Nay Pyi Taw (administrative); Yangon (commercial)

## Legal system overview

Myanmar's legal system reflects layers of legal tradition:
1. **Common law** — inherited from British colonial rule; English common law
   principles remain influential
2. **Customary law** — Buddhist law and indigenous customary practices,
   especially in family and land matters
3. **Constitutional law** — 2008 Constitution (current); prior 1947 and 1974
   Constitutions
4. **Mixed court structure** — Supreme Court, High Courts, and Township Courts;
   separate system for military justice

## Official gazettes

- **Official Gazette of Myanmar** — promulgation of laws, rules, and notifications

## Numbering conventions

Myanmar legislation uses:
- **Law No. X/YYYY** format (e.g., Law No. 15/2017)
- **Year + sequential number** within each category
- **Rules** and **Notifications** follow parent Acts

## Official languages

- **Burmese (Myanmar)** is the sole official language of legislation
- **English** translations exist for some investment and commercial laws
- **Bilingual support:** Limited official English versions

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Some commercial and investment laws have English translations
- Most legislation is authoritative only in Burmese

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "MM"`
- Verify source against Official Gazette or Union Attorney General's Office
- Treat Burmese texts as authoritative; translations as secondary
- Post-2011 legal reforms produced many new laws; verify currency carefully

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Myanmar legislation** from **giving
legal advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate law numbers, article numbers, or legal status.
