## ASEAN legal research workflow (summary)

For cross-jurisdiction research, follow the 16-step workflow in
`legal-research-workflow.md`.

### Quick reference

1. Identify jurisdiction and verify ASEAN membership status
2. Determine applicable legal system (civil law vs. common law vs. mixed)
3. Identify primary source portal
4. Search for legislation by Act/law number or title
5. Verify currency (current vs. amended vs. repealed)
6. Retrieve official text or authorized translation
7. Cross-reference with secondary sources (Globalex, academic guides)
8. Check for ASEAN treaty obligations
9. Verify domestic implementing legislation
10. Validate citation format per jurisdiction
11. Assess evidentiary weight (authoritative vs. secondary)
12. Flag bilingual/multilingual considerations
13. Check for customary/religious law overlay
14. Verify court structure and precedent system
15. Document provenance (source, URL, retrieval date, trust level)
16. Distinguish retrieval from legal advice
