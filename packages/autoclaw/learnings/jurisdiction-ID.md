# Indonesia — Legal System & Sources

## Identity

- **ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:** `ID`
- **Official name:** Republic of Indonesia (Republik Indonesia)
- **Legal tradition:** Civil law (Dutch-derived), with significant customary
  law (Adat) and Islamic law influences
- **Official language:** Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)
- **Capital:** Jakarta

## Legal system overview

Indonesia's legal system is based on **Dutch civil law** (Burgerlijk Wetboek
influence) with layers of:
1. **Customary law (Hukum Adat)** — traditional village/customary law,
   especially in land, family, and inheritance matters
2. **Islamic law (Syariah)** — applied in Aceh province for personal matters
3. **Constitutional law** — 1945 Constitution (UUD 1945), amended multiple times
4. **Mixed courts** — religious courts, administrative courts, military courts

## Official gazettes

- **LN (Lembaran Negara)** — State Gazette for Acts and Government Regulations
- **TLN (Tambahan Lembaran Negara)** — Supplement to the State Gazette

## Numbering conventions

Indonesian legislation uses **year + number + type** conventions:
- **UU No. X Tahun YYYY** — Law (Undang-Undang)
- **PP No. X Tahun YYYY** — Government Regulation (Peraturan Pemerintah)
- **Perpres No. X Tahun YYYY** — Presidential Regulation (Peraturan Presiden)
- **Permen No. X Tahun YYYY** — Ministerial Regulation (Peraturan Menteri)
- **Numbering** is sequential within each type per year

## Official languages

- **Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)** is the sole official language
- **Regional languages** (Javanese, Sundanese, etc.) have no official legislative status
- **English** translations exist for some commercial and investment laws
- **Bilingual support:** Limited official English versions; most texts are
  authoritative only in Indonesian

## Bilingual / multilingual support

- Investment laws and some commercial statutes have English translations
- JDIH provides search in Indonesian only
- Third-party translators provide unofficial English versions

## Retrieval guidance

- Query for `jurisdiction: "ID"`
- Primary source: JDIH Nasional (jdihn.go.id)
- Verify legislation type (UU, PP, Perpres, Permen, etc.)
- Use year + number for exact Act retrieval
- Cross-reference with Globalex guide for access procedures

## Legal safety boundary

The system distinguishes **retrieving Indonesian legislation** from **giving
legal advice**. When a provision cannot be verified, the system returns:
> *"Insufficient verified evidence."*

Never fabricate law numbers, article numbers, or legal status.
